# FAQ: Unit 0.2 (How the curriculum and grading loop work)

## Why does marking a unit done not count as progress? {#progress-vs-verification}

Saying you finished a unit is a claim, not evidence. Code that ran once on your machine can still crash on the next input or drop records. Every submission is executed instead: automated checks run your code in a container and require every test, schema, and exit code to pass, and rubric review then scores it against the unit's rubric, quoting the line that decided each verdict. Gate units, phase projects, and the capstone add further stages on top of those two.

## Why does Phase 11 start in week one instead of after the technical phases? {#parallel-business-track}

Scoping, pricing, and selling AI engineering work takes months of practice, so Phase 11 runs alongside Phases 1 through 10 rather than after them. If you delay it until the technical capstone is done, you finish with no outreach underway and no client pipeline, and the momentum you built stalls. Running it in parallel means your positioning, lead qualification, and scoping skills develop at the same pace as your technical ones.
