# FAQ for Unit 0.1 (Meet the client: OmniSupply Operations)

## Why was my problem statement flagged for using buzzwords? {#buzzwords-trap}

The rubric requires stating OmniSupply's problem without using terms like AI, LLM, machine learning, or agents. Technical buzzwords describe tools, not business problems. Focus entirely on the operational reality: 4,000 monthly transactions arriving across invoices, packing slips, receiving dock photos, and dispute emails, requiring 2 to 3 business days of manual cross-referencing by operations specialists. Stating the problem in plain operational language forces you to identify the true bottlenecks before choosing an architecture.

## How do I resolve conflicts between supply chain auditability and operations speed? {#stakeholder-conflict}

Speed and contract auditability often pull in opposite directions. An operations manager wants immediate dispute triage and credit memos, while a compliance officer requires verifiable supplier contract and SLA citations for every price adjustment. In your brief, do not compromise one for the other. Define speed metrics for the operations manager (under 2 hours triage) while establishing strict citation requirements and human review gates for compliance.
