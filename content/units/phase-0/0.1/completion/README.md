# Your turn: write the OmniCart client brief

You just read a client brief for Apex Freight Logistics. Now you will write one for OmniCart. Use the facts below, follow the rules, and fill in the template. Plan for about 40 minutes of your hour. Do not use any tool but a plain text editor and your own head.

## What OmniCart told us

- OmniCart Operations is a 120-person online store and shipping hub. It sells goods from hundreds of brands.
- Each month, OmniCart gets about 4,000 return requests, damaged parcel claims, and payment disputes.
- Today, a return request waits 2 to 3 days before a clerk looks at it.
- The target is a decision in under 1 hour, with zero lost tickets.
- Sarah Jenkins, VP of Operations, wants the wait cut from 2 to 3 days to under 1 hour, and no ticket lost.
- The CFO wants every refund and every charge tracked in whole cents, in a record no one can change.
- The Trust and Safety Officer wants every return to follow the 14-day return rule. Every damage claim must have photo proof.
- To decide a case, a clerk reads four papers. They are the order receipt, the delivery slip, the unboxing photo, and the return policy.
- Money is always written in whole cents. So 45.20 dollars is written as 4520 cents.
- Every claim has an id like CLM-20841. Every order has an id like ORD-8821.

## The rules

1. Your brief must be 300 to 500 words.
2. Use these five headings, spelled exactly like this and in this order:
   `# OmniCart Operations: Client Brief`, `## The problem`, `## Who cares and why`, `## How it works today`, `## How it should work`.
3. Under The problem, write three sentences or fewer.
4. Under Who cares and why, write one line per leader. The three lines must each want a different thing.
5. Under How it works today, write 4 to 7 numbered steps. Start at the return request. End at the refund paid or denied.
6. Under How it should work, write 4 to 7 numbered steps. Start and end at the same two points.
7. Write at least one amount in whole cents, such as 4520 cents.
8. In How it should work, a person must still review the hard cases.
9. Do not use these words: AI, agent, LLM, model, prompt, automation. One hit and the brief fails. Say what should happen, not what tool does it.
