OmniSupply Operations handles about 4,000 transactions a month arriving as scanned invoices, packing slips, damage photos, and dispute emails. Specialists spend two to three days manually matching line items against purchase orders and supplier return terms before anything gets triaged, which stalls merchant credit memos.
Success means three different things: operations gets disputes triaged in under two hours; compliance gets a supplier contract or SLA citation on every adjustment plus a complete audit log; the CFO gets a fixed cost under $1.50 per transaction.
The workflow validates each incoming document against a schema, extracts and normalises its line items, checks the claim against the governing supplier agreement, and hands a cited recommendation to a specialist for sign-off. The manual steps it replaces are where records currently get dropped and rebate windows expire.
